Blockchain-Based Food Donation Tracking and Transparency System

📌 Project Overview

The "Blockchain-Based Food Donation Tracking and Transparency System" is a decentralized application designed to improve transparency and traceability in the food donation process.

The system uses "Blockchain technology and smart contracts" to record donation-related information in a tamper-resistant manner. It provides a transparent way to track the lifecycle of a food donation from its creation to its status updates.

The project uses "Ethereum Sepolia Testnet" for blockchain transactions and **MetaMask** for connecting the user's wallet.


 🎯 Objectives

- To provide a transparent food donation tracking system.
- To record donation information using blockchain technology.
- To reduce the possibility of unauthorized modification of donation records.
- To allow users to connect their blockchain wallet using MetaMask.
- To track the status of food donations.
- To demonstrate the use of Ethereum smart contracts in a real-world application.


## How Blockchain Is Used

Blockchain is used as the trusted record layer for the donation process.

The basic workflow is:

```text
Donor Wallet
     ↓
Connect MetaMask
     ↓
Create Donation
     ↓
Donation Recorded by Smart Contract
     ↓
Donation Status
     ↓
Status Updated
     ↓
Transparent Donation Record
